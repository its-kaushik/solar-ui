If you're researching rooftop solar for your home in Delhi, the first thing you want to know is: **how much will it cost?** Let's break it down for the most popular system size — 3 KW — which is ideal for most 2-3 BHK homes.

The short answer: after the September 2026 increase in the Delhi government subsidy, **government support now covers most of the cost of a 3 KW home system in Delhi**. Your exact price depends on your roof and the equipment you choose, which is why we quote after a free site survey.

## What Decides the Price of a 3 KW System?

A 3 KW on-grid rooftop system is made up of:

| Component | What affects the price |
|---|---|
| Solar panels (3 KW) | Brand (Adani, Vikram, etc.) and panel technology |
| Inverter (string/micro) | Brand, type, and warranty length |
| Mounting structure | Roof type and how high the structure needs to be raised |
| Wiring, earthing, SPD, lightning arrestor | Distance from roof to meter and safety requirements |
| Installation & labour | Roof access and site complexity |
| Net meter & DISCOM coordination | Handled by us end-to-end |

Two homes asking for "3 KW" can get quite different quotes, so be wary of any price given without someone looking at your roof.

## Government Subsidies You Can Claim

Delhi residents get two subsidies that together cover most of the cost:

### 1. PM Surya Ghar Yojana (Central Government)

For a 3 KW system, you get **Rs 78,000** in subsidy:

- First 2 KW: Rs 30,000/KW = Rs 60,000
- Next 1 KW: Rs 18,000/KW = Rs 18,000
- **Total: Rs 78,000** (disbursed via DBT to your bank account)

### 2. Delhi State Subsidy

In September 2026, the Delhi Cabinet approved a **matching capital subsidy** for home rooftop solar:

- 2 KW system: Rs 52,000
- 3 KW system and above: **Rs 78,000** (disbursed after commissioning)

### Combined Subsidy

| Subsidy | Amount |
|---|---|
| PM Surya Ghar (Central) | Rs 78,000 |
| Delhi State Subsidy | Up to Rs 78,000 |
| **Total Subsidy** | **Up to Rs 1,56,000** |

Faridabad and Gurgaon homes get the Rs 78,000 central subsidy only — the Delhi state subsidy applies to Delhi connections.

## What About EMI / Financing?

You pay the system cost at installation and the subsidy is credited to your bank account afterwards. If you'd rather not pay the full amount upfront, solar loans are available from several banks to bridge that gap. Typical terms:

- **Interest rate:** 7 – 9% per annum
- **Tenure:** 3 – 5 years

We help you apply, and in most cases **your monthly solar savings are larger than your EMI**, so you start saving from month one.

## What's the Payback Period?

With a 3 KW system generating approximately 360 units/month:

- **Monthly electricity savings:** 360 × Rs 7 = Rs 2,520
- **Monthly GBI income:** 360 × Rs 3 = Rs 1,080
- **Total monthly benefit:** Rs 3,600
- **Annual benefit:** Rs 43,200

With up to Rs 1,56,000 of the cost covered by subsidy, **most Delhi families recover their share within about a year**. After that, it's savings for the remaining 24 years of panel life.
